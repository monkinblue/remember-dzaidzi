In Memoriam · Full Colonel (O-6), United States Air Force

Robert “Bob” Martin Buchta

Born July 15, 1939 · Binghamton, New York · “Dzia” · “Dziadek” · “The Dzia Man”

Oldest of ten children on 41 Elm Street. Gannett newspaperboy scholar at Le Moyne College. Air Force flight-test officer, master of engineering management, and program manager for the 30mm ammunition that made the A-10 Thunderbolt II a guardian for American ground troops. Husband to Longina and to Tracy; devoted father to Monica and Stephanie; irrepressible grandfather, golfer, and garage-freezer chalk cartoonist to Robert, Gina, Luke, and Lara.

Exhibition Wing I · Opening Leaves of the Manuscript

“To Those Interested in my Life”

Before answering a single prompt in his 233-page volume, Dzia penned three opening leaves by hand—a warm invitation to his daughters, grandchildren, and unborn great-grandchildren, a formal dedication, and a characteristically candid warning about his opinions.

Folio ii · Dedication Leaf

This journal is dedicated to

my offspring

Robert “Bob” Martin Buchta

R. Buchta

Folio iii · Author’s Preface

A few introductory comments

As you read through this book you will find that I have strong opinions about two subjects — politics and religion. Suprise, suprise!

On these, you may find my ideas too opinionated, too one sided, too rambled, too extreme, or too anything for your taste. That is fine with me. These are my thoughts and so be it!

* Note: Longina died 12-12-90. I may have mistakenly wrote 12-10-90 elsewere in this b[ook.]

Read Verbatim Typeset Transcription of Folio i

To Those Interested in my Life

This book talks mostly of me, but there is also much about our family. If you are a daughter, a grandchild, a great grandchild or.... of mine, then it gives you glimpses of my life and speaks of your heritage.

I suspect that I may have written too much about nothing, so read only what interests you. I hope that you might take with you a few lessons or at least a few ideas to ponder further. Happy Readings!

A loving Father, grandfathe[r], great grandfather....

    Exhibition Wing IV · 328 Lakewood Blvd Garage & Tabletop Archive

    The Freezer-Door Chalk Art Exhibition

    In Maple Bluff, Bob painted the door of his garage Frigidaire freezer with matte black chalkboard paint—transforming every holiday, birthday, Badger game, and family visit into a colorful pastel cartoon laced with unmistakable Dzia puns. Alongside 26 garage freezer-door slates and 3 framed chalkboards, this archive preserves 21 earlier ceramic-marker drawings on his miniature tabletop porcelain easel (2008–2021).

    “For holidays, birthdays, and special visits, the black chalkboard freezer door in the garage became my canvas—chalk dust on my fingers, a joke or pun in block letters, and a cartoon waiting whenever Tracy or the kids pulled into the garage.”

    The Christmas-to-Easter Grinch Re-Hatting (Dec 2019 → Apr 2020)
